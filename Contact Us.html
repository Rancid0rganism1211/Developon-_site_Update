FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services

How quickly can you respond to my inquiry?

We typically respond to all inquiries within 24 hours during business days. For urgent matters, please call us directly for immediate assistance.

Do you offer free consultations?

Yes! We offer free initial consultations to understand your needs and provide tailored recommendations. Contact us to schedule yours.

What areas do you serve?

We primarily serve clients in Accra and surrounding areas for onsite services. For remote services like web development and system monitoring, we work with clients across Ghana and internationally.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept mobile money (MTN, Vodafone, AirtelTigo), bank transfers, and cash payments. For international clients, we also accept PayPal and wire transfers.

Do you provide ongoing support after project completion?

Absolutely! We offer various retainer packages for ongoing support and maintenance. We believe in building long-term relationships with our clients.
