Services that we provide:

School counselors play a crucial role in supporting the academic, social, and
emotional development of students. Their services are diverse and may vary
based on the specific needs of students and the school community. Here are
some common services that school counselors provide:

Academic Counseling

Assisting students in course selection and academic planning. Providing guidance on study skills, time management, and
organization. Helping students set and achieve academic goals.

College and Career Guidance

Assisting students in exploring career options and setting career goals. Assisting with college applications, including guidance on essays and interviews.

Crisis Intervention

Offering support during times of crisis, such as family issues, grief, or emergencies. Collaborating with other professionals to ensure the safety and well-being of students.

Character Education
and Life Skills

Providing guidance on character development and values. Teaching life skills, including communication, decision-making, and problem-solving.

Personal and Social Development

Addressing social and emotional challenges faced by students. Conducting individual and group counseling sessions. Promoting positive interpersonal relationships and conflict resolution.

Support for Special Populations

Offering assistance to students with special needs or learning differences. Collaborating with special education teams to provide inclusive support.
